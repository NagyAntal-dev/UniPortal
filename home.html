The journey

One account from the first form to the last semester

The same profile carries a person from applicant to enrolled student — no re-registration, no second system.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Programme catalogue, online form, document upload and the mathematics placement test.

  2. 02

    Interview & review

    Book a slot in the interview calendar; reviewers score against an agreed set of criteria.

  3. 03

    Offer & payment

    Conditional acceptance letter as PDF, and a unique payment reference so every transfer finds its applicant.

  4. 04

    Visa & enrolment

    Visa checklist, messages with the International Office, and the move to student status.

  5. 05

    Study

    Courses, start-of-term goals and the anonymous end-of-term course evaluation.

  6. 06

    Campus life

    The campus feed, dormitory and maintenance requests, a personal calendar and the university shop.

Who it's for

One platform, five perspectives

Every role gets its own focused menu — on shared data, with access decided by the database, not the screen.

Teachers

  • Own courses and student lists
  • Evaluation results above the privacy threshold
  • Reply to feedback in a comment window

Agents & partners

  • Submit and follow your applicants
  • Commission periods and statements
  • Agency approved before any data is shown

University staff

  • Admissions, interviews and decisions
  • Students, teachers, campaigns and the shop
  • Permissions by role, group or person

Privacy & security

Built for a public university

The rules are enforced where the data lives, not only in the interface.

Anonymous course evaluation

Answers carry no name, and results appear only above a minimum number of respondents.

Access decided by the database

Role, group and individual permissions; a hidden menu item is never the only lock.

Consent log

Every accepted notice and consent is recorded with its version and can be withdrawn.

Payments to the university

Transfers go to the university's own account with a checkable reference. Card payment via SimplePay is coming.

Every account approved

New registrations and partner agencies are reviewed before they see any data.

Hungarian and English

The whole interface switches language; course questionnaires follow the language of the programme.
